# Logo and Favicon

> Provenance: Logo rules come from the written Logo page. The Favicon page contains a single graphic with no written guidance, so Favicon rules are minimal. Statements marked **(Derived)** are inferences.

## Logo

The company logo is the cornerstone of the brand presence and can be integrated into multiple components.

### Two marks

| Mark | Definition | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Logotype** | A unique typographic treatment of the company name | Primary full brand mark, for example in navigation or headers |
| **Isotype** | A symbol for enhanced brand recognition, visually distinct and memorable | Compact situations where the full name does not fit **(Derived)** |

### Variants

| Property | Values |
|---|---|
| Style | White, Black, Brand |
| Size | SM, MD, LG, XL (both marks) |

- Choose the style by background: **White** on dark backgrounds, **Black** on light neutral backgrounds, **Brand** for the colored brand version **(Derived)**.
- Choose the size that matches the container (for example header vs footer); do not scale arbitrarily.

### Placement

- Place logotype and isotype **strategically, considering the focal points** of the design.
- Follow the recommended guidelines for header placement, footer usage or any specific requirement for each application.
- Use the logo in the places defined by the design system (navigation header, footers, landing pages); do not add decorative copies **(Derived)**.

### Alterations

- **Never** make unauthorized alterations, distortions or modifications to the logotype or isotype designs, color or typography.
- Do not stretch, recolor outside the defined styles, change the typography, rotate or add effects **(Derived from the rule above)**.

## Favicon

- The favicon is a small version of the brand symbol for the browser tab.
- It is derived from the isotype shape **(Derived from visuals)**.
- Use the favicon defined in the file; do not substitute a different mark.

## Do and Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use the defined Style and Size variants | Distort or recolor the logo |
| Match style to background | Place a logo with low contrast |
| Use Isotype when space is tight | Redraw or retype the logotype |
| Keep one logo per placement | Repeat the logo for decoration |
